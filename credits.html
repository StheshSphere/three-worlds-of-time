Credits & sources

The Three Worlds of Time: The Broken Hourglass. A University of the Witwatersrand CGV group project. Original level layouts, puzzle logic, story, procedural architecture and custom shaders are credited to the project team.

Libraries and adapted code

  • Hash without Sine, David Hoskins (2014): hash12/hash13 in the shared GLSL noise helpers, MIT notice, reproduced in pySSV's published source.
  • Three.js r160 and bundled add-ons: mrdoob and contributors, MIT licence. Includes the simplex-noise implementation credited in its source to Stefan Gustavson and Sean McCullough.
  • meshoptimizer decoder: Arseny Kapoulkine, MIT licence.
  • Pink-noise filter: Paul Kellett, economy-filter coefficients used in the synthesised ambience. Source permission remains for manual review; no explicit licence was located in the cited text.

Character and environment

  • KayKit Character Pack: Adventurers 1.0 by Kay Lousberg, CC0. The Mage was adapted into Ari: hat/cape/props removed, coat recoloured; the team added goggles, hourglass pack and chrono-gauntlet. Asset pipeline pins source commit 672074b73ba276876a19e8816ecdc5241817ab47.
  • Poly Haven models and PBR textures, CC0. Simplified models and WebP textures are included locally. Individual source asset names are listed below.
