02The team’s contribution

The project-specific design and implementation below belong to the group’s work, developed with the assistance disclosed below and built on the credited libraries and assets.

Three connected worlds

The Broken Hourglass story, Ari’s journey, era progression, level layouts and puzzle sequences: the Past’s light puzzle, the Present’s investigation and power grid, and the Future’s timing and sequence challenges.

Gameplay and interface

Movement and camera integration, interactions, objectives, checkpoints, save and continue, journal, manual dialogue controls, menus and game-state flow.

Procedural world building

The hierarchical Time Machine, architectural geometry, signs and glyphs, procedural city and traffic, plus Ari’s added goggles, hourglass pack and chrono-gauntlet.

Visual and audio integration

Custom shader effects for time warp, reveal-ink, barriers, water, grass, holograms, beams and the sky; lighting, era grading and synthesised ambience. Shared hash functions, noise utilities and reflection/post-processing components are credited below.

The hero’s base model and animations, imported props, PBR textures, recorded music and sound effects are external work. The following credits identify their creators, sources, licences and the team’s adaptations.

04Character, models & textures

Ari: adapted character and animations

KayKit Character Pack: Adventurers 1.0 by Kay Lousberg · CC0 1.0 Universal. The Mage model and supplied animation clips form the basis of Ari, stored as assets/models/hero/scientist.glb.

Adaptation: hat, cape and original props removed; clothing recoloured into a lab coat. The team added goggles, an hourglass pack and a chrono-gauntlet, and integrated the animations into the player controller. The asset pipeline pins source commit 672074b73ba276876a19e8816ecdc5241817ab47.

Environment assets: Poly Haven

Models and PBR texture sets by Poly Haven and its contributing artists, used under CC0 1.0 Universal (Poly Haven asset licence). Each source page below identifies the original asset and its artist information.

Adaptation: props are simplified to scene-specific triangle budgets, their textures compressed, and meshes packed into GLB files. Standalone texture sets are resized to 1024 × 1024 and encoded as WebP. Each set contains color.webp, normal.webp and arm.webp (ambient occlusion, roughness and metalness).

Adapted examples and historical references

David Hoskins’ Hash without Sine is the identified adapted shader source. Its author, source and MIT notice are recorded in the code section. The Three.js noise utility retains its Gustavson / McCullough attribution.

Earlier versions used Paul Kellett’s pink-noise filter, published by MusicDSP. Its coefficient implementation has been removed from the final game and replaced by sample-rate-aware low-pass noise synthesis. This entry acknowledges the historical reference without claiming a licence for the removed implementation.
