      Credits

      Game

      The Three Worlds of Time: The Broken Hourglass — a COMS3006A/COMS3025A Computer Graphics & Visualisation group project, University of the Witwatersrand.

      Level design, puzzles, gameplay code, custom GLSL shaders (sky, time-warp post-process, force field, energy barriers, phase platforms, reveal-ink, water, grass, holograms, light beam), the Time Machine model, all architecture and the procedural city — built by the team. Built with the assistance of AI tooling (Claude Code, Anthropic, Claude Opus) for code generation and editing.

      • Hero character & animations — based on “Mage” from KayKit Character Pack: Adventurers 1.0 by Kay Lousberg — CC0 — kaylousberg.com · github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets (hat, cape and props removed, robe recoloured into a lab coat; goggles, hourglass pack and chrono-gauntlet modelled by us)
