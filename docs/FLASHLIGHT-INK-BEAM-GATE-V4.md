# Present Archive — flashlight-gated ink (V4)

## Intended puzzle

- The four ink marks remain hidden until a flashlight beam touches them.
- Once the flashlight is collected, **left and right arrow keys turn the view/beam**, without moving the character. Mouse look works as before. A/D still strafe. Other eras keep their original left/right arrow movement.
- The green E prompt appears **only** when a mark is in the player's accessible clue circle **and** the beam is actually aimed at it, within a 9m beam range and a narrow central cone.
- Press E while illuminated to read that digit, save it in the journal, and advance the objective. Merely standing nearby, switching F on, or leaving the beam on it does **not** collect it.
- Extra glints do not reveal hidden marks; they appear only during illumination.
- All other clue categories keep the V3 easy invisible 6m-radius E zones; other puzzles and locks are unchanged.

## Quick test

1. Enter the Present. Take Professor Adeyemi's flashlight; press F to ensure it is on.
2. Go to the north dark Archive. Stand near a wall with an ink mark.
3. Point the beam away using left/right arrows. There must be **no** E prompt for that mark; E must not discover it.
4. Turn the flashlight onto the wall mark. The ink should glow, and the green E prompt should appear.
5. Press E to collect it. The counter advances by **one** and J shows the digit and dot position.
6. Sweep away: no more E prompt for this mark. Repeat for all four marks.
7. Check the Past tablets and the office flashlight remain straightforward proximity E items.

Version proof: `BEAM-INK V4 · AIM + E` on the upper-right HUD.
